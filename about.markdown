# About

I'm Yanbin Liu. I lead digital platform work in Sydney — the systems people are supposed to use, and the standards they're supposed to follow.

Most of my career has been spent on the gap between those two things. Organisations write down how work should be done, publish it carefully, and then watch the work happen some other way. That isn't a discipline problem. It's usually an architecture one: the standard lives in a system nobody is in when the work happens.
