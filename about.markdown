# About

I'm Yanbin Liu — a Platform Tech Lead in Sydney, currently at Cochlear.

My work sits across **digital transformation and adoption**, **AI tooling**, and **solution design**. In practice those turn out to be the same problem viewed from three angles: getting platforms into people's hands, getting the work done the way it was actually designed to be done, and — more recently — getting AI agents to help with that without quietly inventing their own version of the rules.

## What this site is about

Enterprise AI tends to get written about from two unhelpful directions: vendor demos that assume a clean slate, and think-pieces that never touch a running system. This site aims at the middle — what actually happens when you put an agent inside an organization that already has standards, owners, permissions, and a change process it isn't going to abandon for you.

Recurring subjects:

**Governed AI workflows.** Where knowledge lives, who owns it, whether an agent can reach the current version at the moment of work, and how you prove it did. Internal knowledge treated as a service rather than as documentation nobody reads.

**MCP, skills and harnesses.** Build notes on the plumbing — connectors that read live sources instead of copies, deterministic checks wrapped around probabilistic models, and the difference between an agent that answers once and one that keeps working until a standard is satisfied.

**Digital adoption at enterprise scale.** WalkMe and DAP, request intake, solution design, and why the bottleneck is almost never the build.

**The boring half.** Permissions, version history, change control, audit trails, failure modes. The parts that decide whether a platform or security team will put their name to it — and the parts most write-ups skip.
